Tire Change

The Tire Change chapter of Builder 101 – Tire Change · extracted from the book's Step Manager plan

Book: Builder 101 – Tire Change Chapter: Tire Change Steps: 11 Sequencing: Step Manager (StateEquals / Named State Machine)
# Step What to do Completion signal Voiceover (to write)
1 Raise Lift Operate the lift to raise the vehicle until the flat tire is clear of the ground. Lift Raised —
2 Grab Wrench Pick up the lug wrench from the tool area. Wrench Grabbed 1 —
3 Remove Lug Nuts Loosen and remove each lug nut from the flat tire with the wrench. Lug Nuts Removed —
4 Replace Wrench Set the wrench back down in the tool area. Wrench Replaced 1 —
5 Remove Old Wheel Pull the loosened wheel straight off the hub and set it aside. Old Wheel Removed —
6 Place New Wheel Lift the replacement wheel and align it onto the studs, seating it flush against the hub. New Wheel Placed —
7 Replace Lug Nuts Hand-thread the lug nuts back onto the studs to hold the new wheel in place. Lug Nuts Replaced —
8 Grab Wrench 2 Pick the lug wrench back up. Wrench Grabbed 2 —
9 Tighten Lug Nuts Tighten each lug nut firmly with the wrench, working in a star / crossing pattern. Lug Nuts Tightened —
10 Replace Wrench 2 Set the wrench back down in the tool area. Wrench Replaced 2 —
11 Lower Lift Operate the lift to lower the vehicle back to the ground, completing the procedure. final step —
